# Storylines

Picked: **A**. All three: 55–65 s, 16:9, music only with on-screen text, iso-glow look, real captures of the app
and real manual pages.

## A. The page comes alive (picked)

Object-to-object chain: every shot ends on the thing that opens the next. The drawing in the manual becomes the 3D
panel, the panel becomes the app, the app becomes the corner combination.

- First 2 s: the real BILLY step 4 page fills the frame; the drawn panel lifts off the paper as the glowing 3D panel.
- Beats: hook (manual page) → tension ("16 pages. Zero words.", pages riffle) → reveal ("Assembly Guide") →
  proof (Showroom orbit, Assembly steps, part zoom on a cam lock) → payoff (the corner combination assembles from
  4 manuals) → claim ("Any manual. Any product.") → what's next (support, store screens, AR, part check) → end card.
- Look: iso-glow dark panel alternating with the white manual paper; the accent only on the moving part.
- Sound: light electronic bed; a soft click when a part seats.

## B. Before and after

Split screen to one: the paper manual (with IKEA's own puzzled-person drawings) next to the 3D guide solving the same
step; the paper side drops away and the app fills the frame. Calmer on the paper side, the beat starts when the app
takes over.

## C. One dowel's journey (the bold one)

One continuous take following dowel 101351 from the parts list into the 3D scene, seating in slow motion, then the
camera pulls back through the bookcase to the whole corner combination and out to the possibilities. Shows the
Showroom and How-it-was-made less.
